## Full description (~2,300 chars)

Car Radio is a walkie-talkie for the road. Push to talk, and your voice reaches the
drivers around you — same road, same direction, same traffic. Hear a heads-up about the
pothole ahead, the lane that's actually moving, or the sunset worth looking at, from
people seeing the same windshield view as you.

EYES UP, HANDS ON THE WHEEL
Car Radio is built to be used without looking at it. The screen is just a status light —
everything happens by ear and by touch:
• Tap anywhere to talk, swipe down to skip a message
• Say "Hey Radio" to talk hands-free, "Hey Radio, mute" or "repeat" for control
• Your steering wheel media buttons work: play/pause to talk, next-track to skip
• Sound cues tell you what's happening — no reading required
• Android Auto: a minimal radar dashboard on your car display, no maps, no feeds

SOUND TRAVELS WITH TRAFFIC
Messages reach forward, the way a warning should: a fast driver's voice carries miles
ahead; slow traffic keeps chatter local. Oncoming traffic and crossing roads are
filtered out automatically. When the road is quiet, Car Radio replays recent messages
left by drivers who passed through before you — and system alerts chime in with local
weather warnings and points of interest.

DRIVE WITH FRIENDS
Road-tripping as a group? Set a shared convoy code and your crew hears each other and
only each other — nobody else can listen in or join.

ANONYMOUS BY DESIGN
No account. No profile. No map of your location. Every trip you get a fresh random
handle like "Neon Falcon," and it disappears when you're done. Messages self-delete
within 24 hours. Nothing about you is stored — see our privacy policy for the full
retention table (everything is deleted automatically, always within 90 days).

COMMUNITY THAT POLICES ITSELF
• Mute any voice with a swipe — they're never told
• Report a speaker by voice ("Hey Radio, report") or a tap
• Repeat offenders are automatically silenced for everyone
• No followers, no likes, no history — just the road, right now

EARLY ACCESS
Car Radio is in active development. It works best where other drivers have it too —
bring a friend, or start your commute's first conversation. Feedback welcome.

Drive safe. Talk soon.
