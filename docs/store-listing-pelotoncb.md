## Full description (~2,000 chars)

PelotonCB turns every phone in your group ride into a voice-activated radio. Join with a
code, put the phone on the bars or in a jersey pocket, and ride. When you speak, it
transmits. When you stop, it listens. No buttons, no screens to look at, no account.

JUST TALK
• Voice-activated: every phrase you say goes out to the pack as a short snippet
• Wind-aware: the mic learns the road noise and only opens for your voice
• Half-duplex, like a real radio: your mic yields while a teammate is talking, so there
  are no echoes and nobody hears themselves twice
• Pause the mic with one giant button or a tap on your earbuds; you still hear the pack
• Works with the mic on Bluetooth earbuds and headsets

YOUR PACK, AT ANY DISTANCE
Enter a code like CLIMB-4821, or mint one and share it from the app. Everyone on the
code hears each other wherever they are, so the rider dropped on the climb still hears
the front of the group, and the front hears them.

OR RIDE OPEN ROAD
No code needed. Open road mode reaches any PelotonCB rider within 500 m heading the same
way as you. Riders ahead, riders behind, riders on the next switchback. Oncoming traffic
is filtered out.

ASK THE ROAD GUIDE
"What's that lake?" "Is there a water stop ahead?" An AI guide answers questions about the
route, the scenery and places nearby, privately, just to you. Everything else you say is
ignored. Switch it off any time.

BUILT FOR THE ROAD, NOT FOR YOUR DATA
• No sign-up, no profile. A fresh rider name every ride, like "Neon Falcon"
• No map of where you are. Location only decides who can hear you
• Voice snippets are deleted automatically within 24 hours
• Mute or report anyone with one tap; they are never told

DAYLIGHT-FIRST, GLOVE-SIZED
A high-contrast handlebar screen with controls you can hit in full gloves. The screen
stays on during a ride. The dial shows at a glance whether you're on air, listening, or
hearing the pack.

EARLY ACCESS
PelotonCB is in active development and works best when your riding partners have it too.
Share a code before the next group ride. Feedback is welcome.

Ride safe. Talk soon.
