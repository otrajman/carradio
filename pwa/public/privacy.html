Car Radio — Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026

Car Radio is built to know as little about you as possible.

No accounts, no identity

There is no sign-up. Each drive ("trip") gets a random phonetic handle (like "Neon Falcon") that is discarded afterward. We collect no name, email, phone number, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

What the app processes

  • Location (GPS position, heading, speed): used on your device to decide which nearby voices you hear. Your live location is never stored on our servers; a position snapshot is attached only to voice messages you choose to send.
  • Microphone: records only while you actively talk (tap, hold, or wake word). Voice messages are short bursts (≤10 s) delivered to nearby drivers.

Automatic deletion

All data is deleted automatically, always within 90 days — most within 24 hours:

DataRetained
Voice messages and their location snapshots24 hours
Messages referenced by a user report30 days (moderation evidence)
Mute events24 hours
User reports30 days
Trip handlesup to 48 hours after their messages expire

Safety and moderation

You can mute or report any speaker; senders are never told. Repeatedly muted or reported speakers are automatically silenced. Reported messages are retained up to 30 days solely for moderation review.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising. Data is processed on Supabase (our hosting provider) solely to operate the service. Optional local alerts use public data sources (US National Weather Service, Wikipedia).
